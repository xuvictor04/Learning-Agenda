under-funded somewhere and you are pretending otherwise.

annual review, after its literacy artifact exists. The first promotions land
around year 6, so this table stops being all blanks much earlier than it used

## How to use this file

Update it at every annual review, and whenever a domain completes — those
are the only two triggers you need. The rest of the year, leave it alone.

Never delete history. A domain that got demoted, a habit that broke, a
promotion that didn't work out — ~~strike it through~~ and write the new
line underneath. The struck-through lines are the part the annual review
actually learns from.

Falling five years behind the spine is not failure; it is the schedule
working as designed (`../03-spine.md`). A tracker with honest gaps is more
useful than one that looks good.
