# Your Knowledge Map — The Polymath Edition

**Declared ambition:** everything — all of it that one mind can hold.
**Current stage:** graduate studies (agenda year 1 ≈ 2026).

Taken seriously, "everything" is a *portfolio problem*, not a single spike.
Nobody masters all fields; but over 40 years a disciplined learner genuinely
can be **world-class in 2–3 fields, professionally competent in 5–8, and
educated-literate in every major domain of human knowledge**. That's what this
map plans for. The enemy is not ambition — it's the polymath failure mode:
surveying everything, mastering nothing. So the map is built on depth tiers.

## The three depth tiers

| Tier | Meaning | Rough cost | Slots |
|------|---------|-----------|-------|
| **T1 — Mastery** | Original contribution; among the best in your context | 5–10 yrs each, sustained | 2–3 per lifetime |
| **T2 — Professional** | Could be paid to do it; can read the field's primary literature | 1–3 yrs each | 5–8 per lifetime |
| **T3 — Literacy** | Know the field's core questions, its 3–5 big ideas, its canon; can follow an expert talk and ask a non-embarrassing question | 100–300 hrs each | unbounded — this is where "everything" lives |

Everything below is assigned a tier. Promotions between tiers happen at annual
reviews, deliberately — never by drift.

## Curiosity budget

For a polymath the whole map is curiosity — so this budget means something
stricter here: **10–20% of learning time on whatever pulls *this month*,
off-ledger, no tier, no artifact required.** It's the release valve that
keeps the tier system from turning joy into bookkeeping.

## Anti-goals (the polymath's guardrails)

- **Not** pursuing any new T1/T2 commitment while a current one is mid-arc —
  new fields enter through T3 only.
- **Not** counting passive consumption (podcasts, videos, feeds) toward any
  tier — literacy requires the artifact.
- **Not** learning tools/frameworks speculatively (principle 5) — the map is
  fields, never products.
- **Not** letting breadth excuse the graduate field: spike 1 slips for
  nothing. A polymath with no spike is an audience member.
