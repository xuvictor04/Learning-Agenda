# Week 1 — the instrument

**Theme.** Before any subject, the things that make every subject cheaper:
how you read, how you remember, how you find out whether you understood.
The only week that's about method rather than material. It pays for itself
by roughly week six.

Days get proposed one at a time, as you ask. Verdicts and outcomes are
recorded here and rolled into `../LOG.md`.
