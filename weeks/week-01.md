# Week 1 — proposal

**Theme: the instrument.** Before any subject, the things that make every
subject cheaper. This is the only week that's about method rather than
material; it pays for itself by roughly week six.

*Status: awaiting your verdicts.*

| # | Proposed | Why | Hrs | Verdict | Done |
|---|----------|-----|-----|---------|------|
| 1 | **Adler & Van Doren, *How to Read a Book*** — Part One + the inspectional-reading chapters (~100pp, not the whole book) | The instrument everything else runs on. Knowing which mode a book deserves is most of the skill | ~2 | — | — |
| 2 | **The inspection exercise** — take any nonfiction book you own, 45 minutes, write five sentences: thesis, structure, evidence, who it's for, read-or-skip | Using it once beats finishing the book | ~1 | — | — |
| 3 | **Anki set up + Woźniak's "Twenty Rules of Formulating Knowledge"** (free, 20 min). Rules 1–4 before your first card | Skipping them produces a year of cards you delete | ~0.5 | — | — |
| 4 | **Ten cards, your own words, from your own reading.** Then 15 min daily | Downloaded decks teach recognition, not understanding | ~1 + daily | — | — |
| 5 | **Publish 300 words, badly, by Sunday** — anywhere public, your name on it | Destroys the standard that would otherwise stop you in week six | ~1 | — | — |

**Total: ~6 hours.** Deliberately light. Starting three habits at once is
harder than it looks, and week 1's real job is that they exist at all.
