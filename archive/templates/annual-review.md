# Annual Review — YEAR

> Copy to `log/<year>-review.md` each December. Do it *before* writing next
> year's plan. Budget 60–90 quiet minutes; skim your weekly logs first —
> answer from the record, not from memory, because memory flatters.

## The record

- **Big investment #1** (from the plan): shipped the artifact? What actually
  happened?
- **Big investment #2:** ___
- **Big investment #3:** ___
- **Habit streaks:** repetition ___ / weekly log ___ (weeks kept vs. total)
- **Finished artifacts this year (list them all, even small):**

## The honest questions

1. **What did I learn fastest this year — and what conditions made that
   true?** (Copy those conditions into next year's plan.)
2. **Where did I put in time but not improve?** What was missing — feedback,
   difficulty, or actual practice?
3. **What did I abandon?** Chosen deliberately, or drifted? (Drift → next
   year's plan needs a structural fix, not a resolution.)
4. **What do I believe about my field(s) that I didn't believe in January?**
5. **Did kill criteria fire, and did I obey them?**
