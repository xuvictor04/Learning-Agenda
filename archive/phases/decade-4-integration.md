# Decade 4 (Years 31–40): Integration and Synthesis

**Mission:** make it one thing rather than forty-four. Re-found the domains
that have moved again, narrow the funding to the depths that earned it, and
do the cross-field work only a long-completed ledger plus two mastered spikes
makes possible.

The ledger completed at year 15 and was repaired once in Decade 3
(`02-map.md`, `03-spine.md`). Twenty-five years of literacy passes and seven to
ten domains at working depth is a position almost nobody holds: most masters
have the spikes without the map, most generalists the map without the spikes.
This decade converts that position into work. It's also, for most people, the
decade of peak professional authority — which is why it needs more structure
than any decade so far. Authority is the easiest thing in this agenda to
mistake for currency.

## Years 31–33: Re-foundation, second pass

**Themes**

- Decade 3's pass took the trunk — the thirty domains of Decade 1. This one
  takes the rest: the fourteen you finished in years 11–15, now twenty years
  old, plus anything in the trunk that moved again while you were re-founding
  it. Take the 5–8 with the largest drift and redo the literacy pass on
  current material — new survey, new canonical work, new artifact. Together
  the two passes complete one full sweep of the ledger.
- Two passes in, the pattern is legible: some domains need refreshing every
  fifteen years and some have not meaningfully moved since you met them.
  Write that classification down. It converts re-foundation from a decade
  project into a rolling maintenance schedule for the rest of the plan, and
  it is the last time the whole ledger needs auditing from scratch.
- **Be taught the new version by people who learned it after you did.**
  Normal and correct, not a concession. The generation that built a field's
  last fifteen years explains them better than anyone who watched from a
  distance, and your job in their classroom is to be a good student.
- Re-foundation isn't remedial or slow. You keep the scaffolding, so you're
  learning the delta rather than the field.
- Check `04-frontier.md` in the same sitting. Some of what looks like drift
  is a field that didn't exist when the map was written, and belongs in the
  frontier slot instead.

**Milestones by year 33**

- [ ] Drift audit written into the year-31 review
      (`templates/annual-review.md`), with the 5–8 domains named.
- [ ] Those domains re-founded on current material, each with a new artifact.
- [ ] At least two of them learned from a teacher who entered the field after
      you did.
- [ ] A written refresh interval for every ledger row — fifteen years, thirty,
      or never — so the next pass is scheduling rather than auditing.

## Years 34–36: The honest narrowing

**Themes**

- You are no longer choosing which domains to promote so much as which
  promotions to keep funding. Seven to ten sit at working depth already, and
  the arithmetic of this decade is subtraction: **one or two new promotions,
  and at least one deliberate demotion.** Ending at 8–12 at working depth is
  the intended shape of a finished plan, not a shortfall.
- Say the trade out loud, because it is the decade's real work: funding ten
  domains at depth means declining thirty-four. Breadth was the mission of
  Decade 1 and half of Decade 2; depth in the places breadth revealed has
  been the mission since. The map doesn't shrink — your funding narrows,
  deliberately.
- Promote for combination, not affection alone: a domain that meets one of
  your spikes at an angle beats two that sit off on their own. Kill criteria
  apply harder here — a promotion that hasn't produced real work in two years
  gets demoted at review rather than defended.
- A domain demoted to literacy is not lost. It stays on the ledger, stays in
  the SRS vault, stays refreshed on its interval. What it loses is a claim on
  the deep hours, and those are the only genuinely scarce thing left.

**Milestones by year 36**

- [ ] 8–12 domains at working depth, each with work you'd show a specialist.
- [ ] The tier table in `02-map.md` rewritten to reflect the narrowing.
- [ ] At least one domain deliberately demoted, and the reason written down.
- [ ] A candidate list for the third spike, kept but unfunded — Decade 5's
      business, not this decade's.

## Years 37–40: The signature work, and building what teaches after you

**Themes**

- **Deep synthesis is this decade's signature output.** Decade 3 built the
  first bridges; this is where you give five years to one of them. Two
  mastered spikes plus a twice-repaired ledger means the questions needing
  both are yours nearly uncontested. Name the two or three you're best
  positioned to ask, pick one, and give the back half of the decade to it.
  Field-defining syntheses tend to get written here precisely because they
  cannot be written earlier.
- **Institutional work begins in earnest.** Decade 3 taught individuals; here
  teaching becomes a build — a course that outlives your teaching it, a
  curriculum, a team, a standard, an open resource. The test is whether it
  still runs in a year you're absent.
- Mentor successors deliberately — not just juniors, but the specific people
  who could carry your best work further than you will. Give the work away
  while you're still around to help.
- Fund, sponsor, or clear the path for younger people the way (you now
  realize) someone once did for you.
- Keep one hard practice with unfakeable feedback alive inside a spike. It's
  the only reliable check on whether your judgment is still calibrated or has
  quietly become taste plus reputation.

**Milestones by end of the decade**

- [ ] One synthesis work published that neither spike alone could produce.
- [ ] Something institutional built and running with other hands on it.
- [ ] At least one named successor carrying work you started, with authority
      to change it.
- [ ] The three permanent habits intact for 40 years.
- [ ] The year-40 review written, and Decade 5's larger hour budget planned
      for *before* it arrives.

## Running through the decade: the instrument, and the money

Principle 8 in `01-principles.md` called the physical substrate learning
infrastructure. Through Decades 1–3 that took care of itself; from here it's
explicit curriculum, scheduled like any other track. The evidence is
unglamorous and solid: **cardiovascular and resistance exercise**, adequate
**sleep**, **social engagement**, **hearing and vision correction**, and
**continued hard learning** are the best-supported protectors of the thing
this agenda runs on. Hearing is the one most often ignored: untreated loss
pulls people out of conversation, teaching, and argument — the exact
activities that keep a mind in use — and it's correctable. Get tested this
decade, not next.

On money: this is typically peak earning, and the `resources/kit.md` items
deferred for thirty years — the lathe, the kiln, the press, the shop built to
your height — become affordable in an afternoon. The year-1 rule holds and is
now harder to obey: buy capability you will use, not capability you'd like to
be the sort of person who owns. If a domain isn't funded at working depth,
its expensive tool is furniture.

## Failure modes to watch for in Decade 4

- **Eminence without production** — the calendar fills with keynotes, boards,
  and opinions about other people's work, and a year passes in which you made
  nothing. Count finished artifacts at every annual review.
- **The frozen model** — teaching your field as it stood in your prime,
  fluently, confidently, and fifteen years late. The re-foundation pass
  exists for this, which is why it's scheduled first.
- **Promoting to avoid choosing** — with the ledger long finished and hours
  available, adding an eleventh working-depth domain feels like ambition. It
  is usually avoidance of the one synthesis that would take five years.
- **Confusing authority with currency** — being deferred to isn't evidence of
  being right. The people best positioned to correct you are now the least
  socially able to; find the ones who will and make it easy for them.
- **Deferring transfer** — "I'll write it down when I have time" is how four
  decades of judgment leave the world with their owner.
- **Narrowing into a single room** — promotions concentrate funding, not
  curiosity. A curiosity budget at zero still means the agenda is dying.
