# Made & Applied

## Engineering: energy & power systems
- **Survey:** *Sustainable Energy — Without the Hot Air* — David MacKay (free
  at withouthotair.com; numbers over slogans, the model of clear thinking
  about energy).
- **Canon:** *Energy and Civilization: A History* — Vaclav Smil (the deep
  account of how energy drives everything else).
- **Course:** MIT OCW *Introduction to Energy Systems* / edX energy tracks.

## Engineering: structures & the built environment
- **Survey:** *Structures: Or Why Things Don't Fall Down* — J.E. Gordon (the
  classic; witty and deep).
- **Canon:** *The Design of Everyday Things* — Don Norman (crosses into design,
  where it belongs too); Vitruvius for the ancient roots.
- **Course:** MIT OCW 1.050 *Engineering Mechanics* for the real thing;
  *How Buildings Learn* — Stewart Brand (book + BBC series) for the systems view.

## Engineering: machines, manufacturing & transport
- **Survey:** *The Way Things Work Now* — David Macaulay (do not skip because
  it looks like a children's book; it isn't).
- **Canon:** *Shop Class as Soulcraft* — Matthew Crawford (the philosophy of
  making and fixing); Henry Petroski, *To Engineer Is Human*.
- **Course:** MIT OCW 2.007 mechanical-engineering design; *Practical
  Engineering* (Grady Hillhouse) video essays for intuition.

## Computing in practice (systems, networks, software)
- **Survey:** *But How Do It Know?* — J. Clark Scott (a CPU from scratch), or
  finish *Code* (Petzold) from the formal cluster.
- **Canon:** *Structure and Interpretation of Computer Programs* — Abelson &
  Sussman (free online; the canon of programming thought); *The Mythical
  Man-Month* — Fred Brooks for software as a human endeavor.
- **Course:** Harvard **CS50** (free on edX) — the best on-ramp to computing
  in existence; *The Missing Semester of Your CS Education* (MIT, free) for
  the practical tools.

## Public health & care systems
- **Survey:** *The Great Influenza* — John Barry, or *Epidemiology: A Very
  Short Introduction*.
- **Canon:** John Snow's cholera study (the founding act of epidemiology);
  *The Emperor of All Maladies* — Siddhartha Mukherjee for medicine-as-system.
- **Course:** Johns Hopkins' free Coursera epidemiology and public-health
  courses (the field's leading school).

## Business, management & entrepreneurship
- **Survey:** *The Personal MBA* — Josh Kaufman (a genuine survey of the whole
  field in one book).
- **Canon:** *The Effective Executive* — Peter Drucker; *High Output
  Management* — Andy Grove.
- **Course:** MIT OCW / Stanford eCorner (free entrepreneurship talks);
  *How to Start a Startup* (Y Combinator, free lecture series).

## Military history & strategy
- **Survey:** *The Art of War in the Western World* or Cathal Nolan's *The
  Allure of Battle* (why decisive-battle thinking misleads).
- **Canon:** Sun Tzu, *The Art of War*; Clausewitz, *On War* (Book I);
  Thucydides, *History of the Peloponnesian War* (the Melian Dialogue).
- **Course:** Yale's *Military History of the West* offerings; the *Hardcore
  History* podcast (Dan Carlin) for narrative immersion.

## Design (industrial, graphic, interaction)
- **Survey:** *The Design of Everyday Things* — Don Norman (the field's front
  door).
- **Canon:** *Thinking with Type* — Ellen Lupton (graphic); *The Timeless Way
  of Building* / *A Pattern Language* — Christopher Alexander (deep and
  cross-disciplinary).
- **Course:** *Design of Everyday Things*-based courses on Coursera;
  IDEO/California College of the Arts design-thinking intros.

## A craft done with the hands
- **Survey/Canon:** none — this box is checked by *doing*, not reading. Pick
  one: cooking (*Salt Fat Acid Heat* — Samin Nosrat), woodworking, ceramics,
  electronics (*Make: Electronics* — Charles Platt), gardening, an instrument.
- **The artifact IS the literacy:** a meal, a joint, a working circuit, a
  harvest. Embodied skill is the one domain the rest of this map can't fake.
