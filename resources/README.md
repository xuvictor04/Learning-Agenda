# Resources

Curated starting materials for every domain in `../02-map.md`, one file per
cluster. These are for the **T3 literacy pass** (survey + canon + artifact);
when a field gets promoted to T2/T1, you'll outgrow this list and find the
field's real curriculum — that's expected and good.

## Selection rules

- **Three slots per domain, not thirty:** a *survey* (the best first pass for
  an intelligent outsider), a *canon* work (the field speaking in its own
  voice — often old, sometimes hard), and a *course* (only when a genuinely
  excellent open one exists). Occasionally a *reference* worth owning.
- **Famous and durable over new and hot.** Everything here has survived at
  least a decade of readers, or is a flagship open course from a major
  university (MIT OpenCourseWare, Open Yale, edX/Coursera flagships).
- **Substitution is fine; skipping the slot is not.** If you prefer a
  different survey of physics, take it — but do read *a* survey, *a* canon
  work, and make the artifact.

## Practical notes

- Editions and platform availability drift; search by title + author. Free,
  stable sources worth knowing: **ocw.mit.edu** (MIT OCW), **oyc.yale.edu**
  (Open Yale Courses), **feynmanlectures.caltech.edu**, **core-econ.org**,
  plus your library and its interlibrary loan.
- The literacy artifact for each domain: a published written summary — what
  the field asks, its 3–5 biggest ideas, what surprised you, what you'd
  study next. One to three thousand words. That artifact, not the reading,
  is what earns the checkmark in the map.
