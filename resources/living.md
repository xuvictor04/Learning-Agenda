# Living

> With a health-sciences background you'll pass the literacy bar on much of
> this already — check those boxes and spend the hours on the ledger's far
> side. Kept here so the map is complete for any reader.

## Evolutionary & molecular biology
- **Survey:** *The Selfish Gene* — Richard Dawkins (the gene's-eye view that
  reorganized the field); pair with *Your Inner Fish* — Neil Shubin for
  deep-time anatomy.
- **Canon:** Darwin, *On the Origin of Species* (the last chapter if not the
  whole); *The Extended Phenotype* (Dawkins) for the advanced statement.
- **Course:** Stanford's *Human Behavioral Biology* — Robert Sapolsky (free
  on YouTube), one of the most loved lecture series online.

## Genetics
- **Survey:** *The Gene: An Intimate History* — Siddhartha Mukherjee.
- **Canon:** Watson & Crick's 1953 *Nature* paper (one page; read it once);
  *The Eighth Day of Creation* — Horace Freeland Judson for the full saga.
- **Course:** MIT OCW 7.00x/7.28 molecular biology series.

## Ecology
- **Survey:** *The Song of the Dodo* — David Quammen (island biogeography and
  extinction, as a page-turner).
- **Canon:** *A Sand County Almanac* — Aldo Leopold (the land ethic);
  *Silent Spring* — Rachel Carson.
- **Course:** Open Yale EEB 122 *Principles of Evolution, Ecology and
  Behavior* (Stephen Stearns), free.

## Medicine & physiology
- **Survey:** *How We Live and Why We Die* — Lewis Wolpert, or *The Body: A
  Guide for Occupants* — Bill Bryson.
- **Canon:** *The Youngest Science* — Lewis Thomas (medicine's coming of age);
  William Osler's *Aequanimitas* essays for the profession's voice.
- **Course:** any accredited human-physiology open course; you likely have
  the real thing already.

## Agriculture & food systems
- **Survey:** *The Omnivore's Dilemma* — Michael Pollan.
- **Canon:** *An Agricultural Testament* — Albert Howard (the founding soil-
  and-compost text) or *The One-Straw Revolution* — Masanobu Fukuoka.
- **Course:** *The Land, the Seed, the Table* style offerings vary; a solid
  free option is edX's *Feeding the World* type courses from Wageningen —
  search current catalog.
