# Meaning & Expression

## Philosophy (history of + core problems)
- **Survey:** *Sophie's World* — Jostein Gaarder (the history of philosophy as
  a novel) or *A History of Western Philosophy* — Bertrand Russell (opinionated,
  brilliant, one voice across 2,500 years).
- **Canon:** Plato, *The Republic* and the *Apology*; Marcus Aurelius,
  *Meditations*; Descartes, *Meditations on First Philosophy*. Pick up
  Eastern canon too: the *Tao Te Ching*, the *Analects*.
- **Course:** Oxford's *General Philosophy* — Peter Millican (free lectures);
  Harvard's *Justice* (Sandel) for ethics; the *Philosophize This!* podcast
  as a chronological companion.

## World religions & mythology
- **Survey:** *The World's Religions* — Huston Smith (the sympathetic classic).
- **Canon:** read *from* the traditions — Genesis and the Gospels, selections
  of the Qur'an, the *Bhagavad Gita*, the *Dhammapada*; and *The Hero with a
  Thousand Faces* — Joseph Campbell for comparative myth.
- **Course:** Open Yale RLST 145 *Introduction to the New Testament* and its
  Hebrew-Bible counterpart (Christine Hayes) — rigorous, secular, free.

## Literature (the world canon)
- **Survey:** *How to Read Literature Like a Professor* — Thomas Foster (the
  toolkit), plus *The Western Canon* — Harold Bloom for the map (and argument).
- **Canon:** don't survey — *read* the works. A lifetime shortlist to start:
  Homer's *Odyssey*, one Shakespeare tragedy a year, *Don Quixote*, a Tolstoy
  or Dostoevsky novel, one from your own language's tradition, one in
  translation you'd never otherwise meet.
- **Course:** Open Yale ENGL 300 *Introduction to Theory of Literature* (Paul
  Fry) for the how; Coursera's *Modern & Contemporary American Poetry*
  (ModPo, Al Filreis) is a beloved free deep-reading community.

## Music (theory + history)
- **Survey:** *How Music Works* — David Byrne (what music *is* and does).
- **Canon:** listen structurally — Bach's *Well-Tempered Clavier*, a Beethoven
  symphony, one jazz landmark (*Kind of Blue*), one from a non-Western
  tradition. Reading: Aaron Copland, *What to Listen For in Music*.
- **Course:** Yale's *Listening to Music* — Craig Wright (Open Yale MUSI 112,
  free); musictheory.net for the mechanics; Coursera's *Fundamentals of
  Music Theory* (Edinburgh).

## Visual art & architecture
- **Survey:** *The Story of Art* — E.H. Gombrich (the best-selling art history
  ever written, and deservedly).
- **Canon:** *Ways of Seeing* — John Berger (short, transformative); Vitruvius,
  *Ten Books on Architecture* for the built canon.
- **Course:** Khan Academy's *Smarthistory* (free, superb); MoMA's free
  Coursera courses on modern art.

## Theater, film & narrative media
- **Survey:** *How to Read a Film* — James Monaco.
- **Canon:** Aristotle, *Poetics* (still the root of story theory); watch the
  canon — a silent film, a Kurosawa, a Hitchcock, a Kubrick, one from world
  cinema you'd skip by default.
- **Course:** the *Every Frame a Painting* video essays (free) for visual
  literacy; open film-studies intros for structure.

## Rhetoric & writing (as an art)
- **Survey:** *On Writing Well* — William Zinsser (nonfiction) and *Style:
  Lessons in Clarity and Grace* — Joseph Williams.
- **Canon:** Aristotle, *Rhetoric*; Strunk & White, *The Elements of Style*
  (flawed, foundational); *The Sense of Style* — Steven Pinker for the modern
  update.
- **Course:** any serious writing workshop with real feedback beats any
  online course here — rhetoric is a feedback sport. Failing that, the
  University of Iowa's free *How Writers Write* series.
