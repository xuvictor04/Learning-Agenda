# Universal Fundamentals

The capacities the whole map runs on (the table in `../02-map.md`). Weak rows
here slow down *every* domain, so these resources come before any ledger
domain. Each is a lifelong practice, not a one-time read.

## Writing clearly
- *On Writing Well* — Zinsser; *Style: Lessons in Clarity and Grace* —
  Williams; *The Sense of Style* — Pinker.
- **Practice:** write publicly every week (the blog/notes habit from
  `phases/decade-1-foundations.md`). Feedback > any book.

## Reading difficult material fast
- *How to Read a Book* — Adler & Van Doren (the classic on analytical and
  syntopical reading — directly a method for this whole agenda).
- **Practice:** the literacy artifacts *are* the practice — reading with a
  summary to write forces active reading.

## Math & statistics (working level)
- 3Blue1Brown's *Essence of* series; Harvard **Stat 110** (Blitzstein);
  *How to Lie with Statistics* — Darrell Huff (short, permanent immunity).
- See `formal.md` for the deep versions.

## Speaking & presenting
- *Talk Like TED* — Carmine Gallo; *Confessions of a Public Speaker* — Scott
  Berkun.
- **Practice:** Toastmasters or any recurring venue with an audience;
  record yourself. Teaching (the final exam, per principle 6) doubles here.

## Programming / data literacy
- *Automate the Boring Stuff with Python* — Al Sweigart (free online; the
  gentlest useful start); Harvard **CS50** for depth (see `made-applied.md`).
- **Practice:** automate one real annoyance in your own life; that's the
  whole on-ramp.

## Working with AI tools
- The vendors' own docs and cookbooks (they move faster than any book);
  Ethan Mollick's *Co-Intelligence* for the durable mental model of
  delegating to and verifying AI.
- **Practice:** use AI to accelerate the literacy artifacts themselves —
  draft, critique, quiz you — while verifying everything it claims. Meta-skill
  and multiplier at once.

## Memory technique (SRS discipline)
- *Make It Stick* — Brown, Roediger & McDaniel (the science); the Anki manual.
- **Practice:** 15–20 min of Anki daily, cards in your own words from your own
  study. This is principle 2 of the agenda and the single habit that makes
  breadth retainable.

## Personal finance
- *The Psychology of Money* — Morgan Housel; *The Bogleheads' Guide to
  Investing*; *I Will Teach You to Be Rich* — Ramit Sethi (mechanics).
- **Practice:** automate savings/investing once, early; compounding needs
  decades and you have them.
